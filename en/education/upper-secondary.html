Education

Upper secondary

Years 9–12: two classes a year on the science track (natural sciences) and two on the humanities track (philology).

Tracks and specialisations

Track (profil)SpecialisationClasses per year
Science (real)Natural sciences2
Humanities (umanist)Philology2

All classes are taught in German, following the mother-tongue curricula. The philology track includes world literature and Latin; the science track includes biology, chemistry, physics and computing, taught in the laboratories of Buildings B and C.

Admission to Year 9

Places are allocated through Romania’s national computerised system, according to each candidate’s admission average and listed preferences. Candidates who were not taught in German as a mother tongue in lower secondary first sit a German-language test, held by the college in May. The steps are set out in the Year 9 guide.

Examinations

At the end of Year 12 students sit the baccalaureate, including a paper in German language and literature. In Year 12 they also take the examination for the German Language Diploma (DSD II) of the Kultusministerkonferenz.

Projects and activities

Upper-school students take part in Erasmus+ mobilities, in exchanges with partner schools – such as the Europaschule Troisdorf – in Jugend debattiert and in the school magazine. Years 9 to 12 organise the Fasching carnival in February.
